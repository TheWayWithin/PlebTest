**The Problem with AI Feedback**

LLMs are too helpful. Ask them about your startup idea and they'll tell you it's brilliant — even when it's not.

Over the past few weeks, I've been building PlebTest to solve this. We shipped an AI-powered validation system that's calibrated for skepticism. 40% of generated personas are highly skeptical, 40% are pragmatic realists, and only 20% are supportive.

The anti-sycophancy system forces personas to raise real objections — minimum 2 for supportive personas, 3+ for critics. We run regression tests to make sure they stay honest.

After testing your idea with multiple AI personas, you get a Kill/Pivot/Build verdict with confidence levels and actionable next steps. You can share reports publicly or keep them private.

This won't replace talking to real customers. But it'll help you figure out which ideas deserve that effort before you waste months building the wrong thing.
