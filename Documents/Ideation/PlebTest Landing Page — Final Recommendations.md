### 3. Problem Section — Shorten and Reframe

**Problem:** Current section has uncited stats ("6-12 months," "$30,000+") and dramatic language ("Emotional Devastation") that may reduce trust.

**Fix:** Shorten by ~40% and reframe around the emotional barrier.

**Current (approximate):**
> "Most founders spend 6–12 months building before discovering there's no market. Average failed startup costs $30,000+. Emotional Devastation affects relationships, mental health..."

**Proposed:**
> "The hardest part of validation isn't building — it's picking up the phone. Most founders skip customer discovery because it's awkward, time-consuming, and emotionally draining. So they build first and validate later — if at all. That's why 90% of startups fail: no market need."

---

### 4. Real Examples — Add Credibility Disclaimer

**Problem:** Readers may wonder if the sample verdicts are from real customers.

**Fix:** Add a small note above or below the examples:

> "Sample outputs from simulated persona interviews (not real customers)."

---

### 5. CTA Consolidation

**Problem:** Multiple CTAs ("Join the Waitlist," "See How It Works," "View Full Report") dilute focus.

**Fix:**
- **Primary CTA (everywhere):** "Join Waitlist for Early Access"
- **Remove:** "View Full Report" buttons on verdict cards (non-functional)
- **Keep:** "See How It Works" as secondary anchor link

---

### 6. Risk Score Format

**Problem:** "Risk Score 87/100" is confusing — is 87 good or bad?

**Fix:** Change to: "Risk Score: High (87)"

Format for all verdicts:
- Kill: "Risk Score: High (87)"
- Pivot: "Risk Score: Medium (52)"
- Build: "Risk Score: Low (23)"
