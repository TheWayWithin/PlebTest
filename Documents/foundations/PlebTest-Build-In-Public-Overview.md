## Overview

PlebTest is an AI-powered idea validation tool for founders who can't—or won't—pick up the phone. It uses AI customer personas to simulate market conversations that challenge your assumptions, delivering clear kill/pivot/build verdicts so you stop guessing and start building winners.

**Tagline:** Kill duds. Find winners.

**Key Phrase:** The genius of crowds — without the phone calls.

---

## The Problem

Founders skip customer validation because talking to strangers is painful. They build blind, discover too late nobody wants it, and waste months on the wrong idea.

**The evidence:**
- 42% of startups fail from no market need
- Founders avoid cold calls due to phone anxiety, introversion, or time constraints
- ChatGPT and friends give sycophantic feedback that confirms bad ideas

**The cost of not solving this:**
- 3-6 months wasted building the wrong thing
- Runway burned on features nobody wants
- Winning ideas sit unbuilt while chasing losers

---

## How PlebTest Solves This

PlebTest gives your market a voice—without you having to pick up the phone.

**The solution:**
1. Describe your idea (problem, solution, hypotheses)
2. Define your ideal customer profile (ICP)
3. AI generates diverse personas based on your ICP (varying demographics, psychographics, personality traits)
4. Personas challenge your assumptions through simulated conversations
5. Get a clear verdict: **Kill**, **Pivot**, or **Build**

**What makes it work:**
- **Anti-sycophancy by design** — AI is built to challenge, not flatter
- **Collective intelligence** — Multiple personas find holes no single conversation could
- **Pivot intelligence** — Doesn't just kill bad ideas; shows you how to twist them into winners
- **Speed** — Get honest market feedback in minutes, not months

---

## Who It's For

**Primary:** Technical solo founders (developers, engineers) who would rather code than cold-call. High phone anxiety, paralyzed by rejection fear.

**Secondary:** Side-hustle builders with full-time jobs who can't schedule calls during business hours and need async validation.

**Tertiary:** Domain experts starting their first business—strong expertise, weak confidence, crippling imposter syndrome.

**Where they hang out:** Twitter/X #buildinpublic, Indie Hackers, Hacker News, Reddit

**Who it's NOT for:**
- People who enjoy networking and cold outreach
- Founders who want validation theater for investors
- People with no specific idea yet
- Anyone too defensive to hear the truth
