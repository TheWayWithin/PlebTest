# Vision and Mission

**Business Name:** PlebTest
**Date:** January 8, 2026
**Version:** 3.0

---

## My Vision

**"The fear of customer discovery no longer stops anyone. Founders launch knowing, not hoping."**

Too many good ideas die because their founders are too nervous to pick up the phone. PlebTest changes that by giving founders real answers through AI-powered customer simulations - creating a psychological shield that allows them to validate ideas with brutal honesty and without social anxiety. Move forward prepared, not paralyzed.

---

## My Mission

**"I give founders who dread picking up the phone - or are simply too time-poor - a way to kill bad ideas fast, so they can finally get to the great one."**

PlebTest uses AI-powered customer simulations to stress-test your assumptions with brutal honesty. Spectator Mode means you never have to talk - just watch, learn, and decide. Technology gets you to 'no' faster. Guardrails help you trust the answer. And thoughtful design makes it painless to hear.

## The PlebTest Name

The name 'PlebTest' reflects my core belief: true market genius emerges not from the isolated founder, but from the collective, honest feedback of the masses (the 'plebs'), which my AI simulates at scale.
