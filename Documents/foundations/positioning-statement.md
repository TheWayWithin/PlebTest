## Core Positioning Statement

**For** founders who can't - or won't - pick up the phone, but need to kill bad ideas fast so they can get to the great one,

**PlebTest** gives your market a voice
**that** tells you which ideas to kill, pivot, or build
**so you** stop guessing and start building winners.

**Unlike** forcing yourself through painful cold calls or just asking ChatGPT,
**my approach** is AI customer personas built to challenge your assumptions, not validate them - so you hear real objections before you build the wrong thing.
