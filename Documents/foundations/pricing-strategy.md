# Pricing Strategy

**Business Name:** PlebTest
**Date:** January 12, 2026
**Version:** 1.0

---

## Pricing Philosophy

"Price for the decision, not the tool. Each test that kills a bad idea early saves months of wasted building. We charge a fraction of what one wrong decision costs."

**Pricing Principles:**

1. **Value-based, not cost-based** - Price reflects decisions accelerated, not compute consumed
2. **Low barrier, clear path up** - Solo founders can start at coffee-budget pricing; power users get volume
3. **Usage-aligned growth** - You pay more only when you need more (ideas to test, depth of analysis)
4. **No punishment for success** - Unlimited ideas explored within tier; pay for capacity, not outcomes

| **"Why pay when I can just ask ChatGPT?"** | "ChatGPT is trained to agree with you. PlebTest is built to challenge you. One makes you feel good, the other makes you ready." |
| **"Am I a bad founder for not doing real interviews?"** | "You're a founder who wants to be prepared. That's not lazy - that's smart." |
| **"What if it says my idea is bad and it's wrong?"** | "Then you're out an hour and a coffee. But if it's right, you just saved 3-6 months." |
| **"Is this just validation theater for investors?"** | "Investors see through theater. This gives you real objections to address - that's what they want to hear." |
