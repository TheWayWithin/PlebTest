## Visual Identity

### Color Palette

**Primary Colors:**

| Color | Hex | RGB | Usage |
|-------|-----|-----|-------|
| **Slate** | #1E293B | 30, 41, 59 | Primary text, headings, UI elements |
| **White** | #FFFFFF | 255, 255, 255 | Backgrounds, cards, breathing room |
| **Off-White** | #F8FAFC | 248, 250, 252 | Page backgrounds, subtle sections |
| **Gray** | #6B7280 | 107, 114, 128 | Secondary text, UI elements, borders |

**Accent Colors:**

| Color | Hex | RGB | Usage |
|-------|-----|-----|-------|
| **Amber** | #F59E0B | 245, 158, 11 | CTAs, highlights, "build" signals — use boldly |
| **Red** | #EF4444 | 239, 68, 68 | "Kill" signals, warnings, destructive actions |
| **Emerald** | #10B981 | 16, 185, 129 | "Success" signals, positive indicators |

**Color Usage Notes:**
- Slate over black = serious but not harsh
- Amber accent = warm, approachable — use aggressively in CTAs, social cards, headers
- Functional colors (red/green) map directly to kill/build decisions
- Always run WCAG contrast checker before implementing text on backgrounds

### Typography

**Primary Font: Inter**
- Clean, modern, highly readable on screens
- Free via Google Fonts
- Developer-friendly — common in indie/builder tools

| Use | Weight | Size Guidelines |
|-----|--------|-----------------|
| Headings | Semi-Bold (600) | 24-48px |
| Body | Regular (400) | 16-18px |
| Small text | Regular (400) | 14px minimum |

**Monospace Accent: JetBrains Mono**
- For code snippets, technical elements, or "verdict" displays
- Signals technical credibility without being exclusive
- Use sparingly for emphasis

**Web Fonts:**
- fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter
- fonts.google.com/specimen/JetBrains+Mono

### Imagery Style

**Photography:**
- Minimal or none — avoid stock photos entirely
- If used: real founders, candid shots, not posed corporate
- Skip founder photos unless high quality — weak photos hurt credibility

**Illustrations:**
- Simple line illustrations or icons
- Flat style, not 3D or glossy
- Conveys concepts without cheesiness

**Icons:**
- Line-style, consistent stroke weight
- Sources: Heroicons, Lucide, or Phosphor (all free, dev-friendly)

**Screenshots/Product:**
- Clean, minimal UI shots
- Dark mode option available (developer comfort)

### Logo Direction

**Style:** Wordmark (text-based) or simple mark + wordmark
**Feel:** Clean, confident, slightly technical
**Avoid:** Overly playful, corporate swooshes, generic startup aesthetics
**Consider:** The "pleb" concept — could incorporate subtle crowd/collective imagery

**Usage Guidelines (when created):**
- Maintain clear space (minimum: height of logo "P")
- Don't stretch, rotate, or recolor outside palette
- Don't place on busy backgrounds without container
- Minimum size: 24px height for digital

## Brand Voice

### Voice Characteristics

| We Are | We Are Not |
|--------|------------|
| **Direct** — Get to the point fast | Blunt or cold — no empathy |
| **Honest** — Say the hard thing when needed | Harsh — cruelty disguised as honesty |
| **Supportive** — We're on your side | Cheerleader — empty encouragement |
| **Credible** — Speak from experience | Academic — theory without practice |
| **Casual** — Talk like a person | Sloppy — unprofessional or careless |
| **Confident** — Know what we're about | Arrogant — talking down to people |

**Note:** Casual tone requires strong writing. Weak casual reads sloppy faster than weak formal.

### Tone Variations by Context

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| **Marketing** | Confident, punchy, direct | "Kill duds. Find winners. Without the phone calls." |
| **Product UI** | Clear, neutral, helpful | "Your idea scored 3/10. Here's what they didn't like." |
| **Support** | Warm, patient, solution-focused | "That's frustrating. Let me help you figure this out." |
| **Social Media** | Casual, relatable, real | "Ran my own idea through PlebTest today. Got roasted. Needed it." |
| **Error Messages** | Calm, clear, actionable | "Something went wrong. Try again, or reach out if it keeps happening." |
| **Kill Verdicts** | Honest but kind | See example below |

**Channel-Specific Tone:**

| Platform | Adjustment |
|----------|------------|
| Twitter/X | Punchiest. Short, direct, personality shows. |
| LinkedIn | Slightly more professional, still human. |
| Indie Hackers | Builder-to-builder. Show your work. |
| Product Hunt | Benefit-focused, softer opening, invite discussion. |
| Hacker News | Show methodology briefly. Skews academic. |

### Kill Verdict Example (Honest But Kind)

**Good — builds trust:**
> **Verdict: Kill (3/10)**
>
> The market isn't buying this one. Here's what they told us:
> - Price too high for the value offered
> - Existing solutions already solve this well enough
> - Target audience doesn't see this as a priority
>
> **What you could try:** The need is real, but your angle isn't landing. Consider repositioning around [specific insight from feedback] — that's where interest spiked.

**Bad — destroys trust:**
> **Score: 3/10**
>
> Your idea failed validation. Better luck next time.

The difference between "that's dead" and "this angle won't work, but here's what might" is where trust is built or broken.

### Writing Guidelines

**Do:**
- Start with the point, then explain
- Use "you" and "your" — speak directly
- Short sentences. Short paragraphs.
- Say "I" when it's Jamie speaking (blog, social)
- Acknowledge the hard stuff (killing ideas hurts)
- Give the next step — don't leave people hanging

**Don't:**
- Hedge with "we think" or "perhaps" — be direct
- Use jargon or buzzwords (synergy, leverage, ideate)
- Sound like a press release or corporate memo
- Sugarcoat bad results — but don't be cruel either
- Use exclamation marks constantly — one is plenty
- Make promises you can't keep
- Be performative about "acknowledging hard stuff" — keep it genuine

### Vocabulary

**Words We Use:**

| Word | Why |
|------|-----|
| Kill, pivot, build | Core decision framework |
| Dud, winner | Plain language for outcomes |
| Validate, test | What the tool does |
| Honest, brutal, real | Our differentiator |
| Founder, builder | Who we serve |
| Idea, assumption | What we challenge |

**Words We Avoid:**

| Avoid | Use Instead | Why |
|-------|-------------|-----|
| Entrepreneur | Founder, builder | Less formal |
| Ideate | Come up with, explore | Jargon |
| Leverage | Use | Corporate speak |
| Synergy | — | Never |
| Revolutionary, game-changing | — | Overhyped |
| Easy, simple | Clear, fast | Don't oversell |
| Guaranteed results | — | Can't promise outcomes |

### Social Strategy: Founder-First

**Why founder-first works for PlebTest:**
- Positioning is "practitioner-built" — indie hackers buy the founder first
- Target audience (#buildinpublic) expects authentic founder voice
- Solo founder bandwidth — one strong presence beats two weak ones
- Product accounts can grow out of founder credibility later

**Account Roles:**

| Account | Voice | Content |
|---------|-------|---------|
| @Jamie_within | Personal, "I" language | Build-in-public, lessons, real results |
| @plebtest | Product, "we" language | Feature updates, user testimonials, tips |
| support@plebtest.com | Helpful, solution-focused | Customer support |

**Transition Triggers (when to scale product accounts):**
- Hiring a marketer or community person
- Need distributed voice across channels
- Traction requires separation of personal/business

|---------|-----------|

### Posting Guidelines

**Do:**
- Share real results (your own tests)
- Engage genuinely in replies
- Admit mistakes publicly
- Use plain language
- Show the product in action

**Don't:**
- Hype without substance
- Auto-reply or template responses
- Delete negative feedback
- Hashtag spam (#startup #founder #AI #success)
- Use stock imagery or generic graphics

> Ran my pricing idea through PlebTest today.
>

>

>
> PlebTest is revolutionizing how founders validate ideas!
>
> #startup #founder #AI #innovation #buildinpublic

**Good:**
> Something went wrong loading your results.
>
> Try refreshing the page. If it keeps happening, email support@plebtest.com and we'll fix it.

**Bad:**
> Error 500: Internal Server Exception. Contact administrator.

**Before (generic SaaS):**
> "Leverage our innovative platform to ideate and validate your entrepreneurial journey with cutting-edge AI technology."
