> ## Killed: PlebTest, August 2026
>
> This was AI-simulated customer interviews that returned a kill, pivot or build verdict on a product idea. Parked in the May 2026 triage, retired with the SaaS model, never reactivated: a product for validating ideas that was itself never validated. Everything it ran on was shut down by 2 November 2026.
>
> What it taught me, set against the research on AI customer personas: [Telling AI to act as your customers makes its answer worse](https://jamiewatters.work/journey/ai-fake-customer-interviews).
>
> The code stays public under the MIT licence as the record of what was built. No support, no updates. What follows is the README as it was left.

# PlebTest
PlebTest is an AI-powered idea validation tool for founders who can't—or won't—pick up the phone. It uses AI customer personas to simulate market conversations that challenge your assumptions, delivering clear kill/pivot/build verdicts so you stop guessing and start building winners.
